## In one sentence

<KeyIdea>
  Reading answers does not prepare you to give one. A mock loop makes you **answer out loud, on a clock, with
  follow-ups**, which is the part of the interview that reading skips.
</KeyIdea>

## How a loop runs

1. Choose a company and a level. Questions tagged to that company are drawn first, and no topic repeats until every topic has come up once.
2. For each question, start the timer and answer out loud. The bar shows which part of the answer you should be in.
3. Press **I have answered**, then take the follow-up questions one at a time, out loud.
4. Rate the answer from 1 to 5 and move on.
5. The last screen shows your average and which answers to practise again.

A real behavioral round has five or six questions in about 35 minutes. Five questions here take 15 to 20 minutes.

## Getting more from it

- **Stand up, or sit as you would in the interview.** Speaking in your interview posture changes your pace.
- **Do not restart an answer.** In the interview you cannot. Finish the weak answer, rate it honestly, and run it again tomorrow.
- **Use your ratings.** They are saved, and the question lists in [Interview Practice](/interviews) can be sorted weakest first.
