## In one sentence

<KeyIdea>
  A small set of stories can answer most behavioral questions, as long as you know **which story answers which
  question** before you walk in.
</KeyIdea>

## How to use it

1. Write your stories in the [Story Builder](/behavioral/practice-lab/story-builder). Eight is a good number to aim for.
2. For each row below, tick every story that could answer a question on that topic.
3. Rows marked **gap** have no story. Either one of your stories covers it and you have not ticked it, or you need a new story.
4. Switch the view to check the same stories against the Amazon Leadership Principles or Meta's focus areas.

<StoryMatrix builder="/behavioral/practice-lab/story-builder" />

## Reading the result

- **Aim for two stories per row** in the areas your target company cares about most. Interviewers in the same loop compare notes, and the same story told four times reads as thin experience.
- **One story can cover several rows.** A migration that went wrong can answer questions on failure, on conflict and on delivery. Tick all three, and decide in advance which part you would stress for each.
- **A gap in the failure and conflict rows is the most common one.** Most people prepare their successes first.
