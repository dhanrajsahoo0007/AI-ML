High-craft product and fintech companies—led by **Stripe** and **Airbnb**—run some of the most selective behavioral loops in tech. At Stripe, the **Manager Chat** tests whether you combine **"Users First"** obsession with **extreme operational reliability** (where a subtle race condition or non-idempotent retry can move millions of dollars erroneously). At Airbnb, ** two dedicated Core Values interviews** are conducted by trained bar-raisers completely outside the engineering org to test whether you **"Be a Host"** and **"Champion the Mission."**

<KeyIdea title="Micro-Pessimism, Macro-Optimism & End-to-End Craft">
Stripe's Operating Principles pair **macro-optimism** (ambitious long-term platform bets) with **micro-pessimism** (assuming every network call will drop, every webhook will arrive twice, and every edge case will hit in production). Airbnb pairs **end-to-end user belonging** with **humble, cross-functional creativity**. In both loops, treating your job as "just closing Jira tickets inside the backend boundary" is a fast rejection.
</KeyIdea>

### How Stripe and Airbnb Differ from Big Tech Loops

1. **Stripe's Operating Principles ("Users First", "Think Rigorously", "Trust and Amplify"):**
   - Stripe's **Manager Chat** is a deep project archaeology session + behavioral interview. The manager will pick one complex system from your resume and drill into **idempotency, data integrity, rollout safety, developer ergonomics (DX), and how you talked directly to users** when friction arose.
2. **Airbnb's Core Values Loop (2 Dedicated Cross-Functional Interviews):**
   - Airbnb evaluates four values: **Champion the Mission**, **Be a Host** (caring deeply for teammates, guests, and hosts), **Embrace the Adventure** (thriving in uncertainty with curiosity), and **Be a Cereal Entrepreneur** (scrappy, resourceful problem-solving—named after the founders selling commemorative cereal boxes to fund the company).

- **Result:** We migrated 100% of traffic with **zero ledger discrepancies**, reduced duplicate-hold support tickets by **94%**, and cut median merchant integration time by **32%**.

- **Result:** Last-minute airport-day cancellations dropped by **41%** over the July 4th weekend, saving **$95K/month** in rebooking costs while increasing host satisfaction scores because they weren't hit with surprise penalty fees.
