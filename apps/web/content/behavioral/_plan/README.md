## 1. Core Philosophy: Practical, Interactive & Genuinely Introspective

1. **Interactive Visual Mental Models on Every Page:**
   - 12 multi-step `<AnimatedDiagram stepper>` specs (`architecture`, `sequence`, and `layout` kinds) in `assets/diagrams/animated/behavioral/`.
   - Interactive `<DecisionTree>` diagnostic widgets on pages.
   - Interactive `<Tabs>` and `<Compare>` rubrics contrasting weak vs. strong answers and `Junior` vs. `Mid` vs. `Senior / Staff+` framing side by side.
2. **Genuine Career Introspection (`## Introspect your own work` + `<Quiz>`):**
   - Every page includes a dedicated reflection and blind-spot audit helping candidates inspect their real project history, uncover where they made trade-offs or mistakes, and build authentic stories that survive 3–4 layers of Bar Raiser follow-ups.
3. **100% Originality:**
   - Written from first principles in our own words with our own engineering systems, numbers, and diagrams. External repositories, blogs, and handbooks appear only in `## Further reading` or `refs`.

1. `## In one sentence` (`<KeyIdea>`)
