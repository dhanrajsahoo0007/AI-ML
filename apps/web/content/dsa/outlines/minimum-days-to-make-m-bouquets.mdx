Flower i blooms on bloomDay\[i\]. To make one bouquet you need k adjacent bloomed flowers.
Return the earliest day you can make m bouquets, or -1 if m \* k &gt; n.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
Input: bloomDay = [1, 10, 3, 10, 2], m = 3, k = 1
Output: 3
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= len(bloomDay) <= 10^5
- 1 <= bloomDay[i] <= 10^9
````
