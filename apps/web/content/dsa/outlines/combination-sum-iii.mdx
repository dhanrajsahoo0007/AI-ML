Find all unique combinations of k numbers from 1..9 that sum to n; each digit at most once.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
    Input: k = 3, n = 7
    Output: [[1, 2, 4]]

Example 2:
    Input: k = 3, n = 9
    Output: [[1,2,6],[1,3,5],[2,3,4]]
````

## Constraints

````text
- 2 <= k <= 9
- 1 <= n <= 60
````
