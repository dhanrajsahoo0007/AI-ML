arr is strictly increasing positive integers. Return the k-th positive integer not present in arr.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
Input: arr = [2, 3, 4, 7, 11], k = 5
Output: 9
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= len(arr) <= 10^5
- 1 <= arr[i], k <= 10^9
````
