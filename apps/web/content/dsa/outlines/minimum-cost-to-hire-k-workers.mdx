Given n workers with quality and wage arrays, hire k workers such that:
- Every worker is paid at least their minimum wage expectation.
- Pay is proportional to quality within the group.
Return the minimum total cost.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
    Input: quality = [10,20,5], wage = [70,50,30], k = 2
    Output: 105.0

Example 2:
    Input: quality = [3,1,10,10,1], wage = [4,8,2,2,7], k = 3
    Output: 30.66667
````

## Constraints

````text
- n == quality.length == wage.length
- 1 <= k <= n <= 10^4
- 1 <= quality[i], wage[i] <= 10^4
````
