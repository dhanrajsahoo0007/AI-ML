Given a balanced parentheses string, compute its score where "()" scores 1, concatenation adds
scores, and wrapping "(A)" doubles A's score.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
Input: "()"
Output: 1
````

## Constraints

````text
- 2 <= len(s) <= 50
- s is balanced using '(' and ')'
````
