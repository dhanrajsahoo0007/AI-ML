Every person has two parents in an infinite genealogical graph. Support queries: are two
nodes siblings (share a parent), first cousins (parents are siblings but they are not
siblings), and lowest common ancestor in the parent-closure sense.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
Parents of A are P and Q; parents of B are P and R -> siblings (share P).
````

## Constraints

````text
- Node ids are hashable; parent pointers may be None for roots.
````
