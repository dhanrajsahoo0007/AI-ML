Given an array people where people\[i\] is the weight of the ith person, and an integer limit
such that each boat carries at most two people and their combined weight is at most limit,
return the minimum number of boats needed to rescue everyone.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
    Input: people = [1,2], limit = 3
    Output: 1

Example 2:
    Input: people = [3,2,2,1], limit = 3
    Output: 3

Example 3:
    Input: people = [3,5,3,4], limit = 5
    Output: 4
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= people.length <= 5 * 10^4
- 1 <= people[i] <= limit <= 3 * 10^4
````
