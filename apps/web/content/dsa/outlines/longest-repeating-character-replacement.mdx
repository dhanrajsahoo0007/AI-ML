Given a string s of uppercase English letters and an integer k, you may change at
most k characters to any other uppercase letter. Return the length of the longest
substring you can obtain where all letters are identical after the changes.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
    Input: s = "ABAB", k = 2
    Output: 4
Example 2:
    Input: s = "AABABBA", k = 1
    Output: 4
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= len(s) <= 10^5
- s contains only uppercase English letters.
````
