Remove substrings "ab" for x points or "ba" for y points repeatedly to maximize total score.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
Input: s = "cdbcbbaaabab", x = 4, y = 5
Output: 19
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= len(s) <= 10^5
- 1 <= x, y <= 10^4
````
