Split nums into k contiguous non-empty parts to minimize the largest sum among those parts.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
Input: nums = [7, 2, 5, 10, 8], k = 2
Output: 18
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= len(nums) <= 1000
- 1 <= k <= min(50, len(nums))
````
