Given nums that may contain duplicates, return all subsets without duplicate subsets.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
    Input: nums = [1, 2, 2]
    Output: [[],[1],[1,2],[1,2,2],[2],[2,2]]

Example 2:
    Input: nums = [0]
    Output: [[],[0]]
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= len(nums) <= 10
- -10 <= nums[i] <= 10
````
