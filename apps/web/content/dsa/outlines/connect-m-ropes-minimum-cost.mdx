Given n ropes of different lengths, connect them into one rope.
The cost of connecting two ropes equals the sum of their lengths.
Find the minimum total cost to connect all ropes.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
    Input: ropes = [4, 3, 2, 6]
    Output: 29
    Explanation: 2+3=5 (cost 5), 4+5=9 (cost 9), 6+9=15 (cost 15). Total: 29.

Example 2:
    Input: ropes = [1, 2, 3]
    Output: 9
    Explanation: 1+2=3 (cost 3), 3+3=6 (cost 6). Total: 9.
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= ropes.length <= 10^4
- 1 <= ropes[i] <= 10^4
````
