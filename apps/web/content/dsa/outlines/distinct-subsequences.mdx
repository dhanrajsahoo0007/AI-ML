Given strings s and t, count how many distinct subsequences of s equal t
(modulo 10^9 + 7).

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
    Input: s = "rabbbit", t = "rabbit"
    Output: 3
Example 2:
    Input: s = "babgbag", t = "bag"
    Output: 5
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= len(s), len(t) <= 1000
````
