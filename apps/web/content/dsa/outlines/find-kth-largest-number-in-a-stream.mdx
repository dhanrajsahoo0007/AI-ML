Process a stream via getNum() until None; maintain unique values and return the k-th largest
unique number (1 = largest). Duplicates are ignored for ranking.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
Stream [5,1,5,2,3,2,4], k=3 -> third largest among uniques {1..5} is 3.
````

## Constraints

````text
- At least k distinct values appear in the stream.
````
