Given non-overlacing axis-aligned rectangles (x1,y1,x2,y2), find a vertical line x that
splits total area as evenly as possible (area strictly to the left of x vs strictly to the right).

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
    Input: [(1, 1, 4, 4)]
    Output: 2.5
Example 2:
    Input: [(1, 1, 3, 3), (4, 1, 6, 3)]
    Output: 3.5 (plateau of equal left-area between rectangles; midpoint is used)
Example 3:
    Input: [(1, 1, 3, 3), (3, 1, 6, 3)]  # total area 10, half is 5
    Output: 3.5 (vertical line where cumulative area from the left reaches 5)
````

## Constraints

````text
- Rectangles do not overlap; x2 > x1 and y2 > y1.
````
