Infinite coins of each denomination in coins, but you may not mix denominations in one amount.
Return the k-th smallest positive amount in the multiset of all single-denomination multiples.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
Input: coins = [3, 6, 9], k = 3
Output: 9
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= len(coins) <= 15
- 1 <= k <= 2 * 10^9
````
