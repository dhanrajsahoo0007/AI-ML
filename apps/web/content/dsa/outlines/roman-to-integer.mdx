Given a Roman numeral string s, convert it to an integer. Subtractive pairs such as
IV and CM are valid and must be interpreted correctly.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
    Input: s = "III"
    Output: 3

Example 2:
    Input: s = "LVIII"
    Output: 58

Example 3:
    Input: s = "MCMXCIV"
    Output: 1994
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= len(s) <= 15
- s is a valid Roman numeral in the range [1, 3999].
````
