Robots occupy unique positions with health and move either right or left. When a right-moving robot
meets a left-moving one they collide: lower health disappears, ties destroy both, otherwise the
stronger survives with health reduced by one. Return surviving healths sorted by original index.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
Input: positions=[5,4,3,2,1], healths=[2,17,9,15,10], directions="RRRRR"
Output: [2,17,9,15,10]
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= len(positions) <= 10^5
- directions matches positions length and uses only 'L' or 'R'
````
