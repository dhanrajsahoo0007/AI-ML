Given a string s and an integer k, return the length of the longest substring such
that every character in that substring appears at least k times.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
    Input: s = "aaabb", k = 3
    Output: 3
Example 2:
    Input: s = "ababbc", k = 2
    Output: 5
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= len(s) <= 10^4
- 1 <= k <= len(s)
````
