Toys lined on a shelf each have a category id. You may pick at most k different
categories at a time while taking a contiguous block. Return the maximum number of
toys you can pick.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
    Input: toys = [1, 2, 1], k = 2
    Output: 3
Example 2:
    Input: toys = [1, 2, 1, 2, 1], k = 2
    Output: 5
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= k <= number of distinct categories present in the input (practically k <= len(toys))
````
