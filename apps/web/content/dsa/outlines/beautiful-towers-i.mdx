Given max heights per index, choose actual heights (&lt;= caps) forming a mountain (non-decreasing then
non-increasing) to maximize the total sum.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
Input: [5, 3, 4, 1, 1]
Output: 13
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= len(heights) <= 10^3
````
