Given a string s of words separated by spaces, return the length of the last word.
A word is a maximal substring of non-space characters.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
    Input: s = "Hello World"
    Output: 5
Example 2:
    Input: s = "   fly me   to   the moon  "
    Output: 4
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= len(s) <= 10^4
- s consists only of English letters and spaces.
- There is at least one word in s.
````
