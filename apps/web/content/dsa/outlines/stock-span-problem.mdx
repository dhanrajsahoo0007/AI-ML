Implement StockSpanner.next(price) returning the span of consecutive days ending today where prices
were less than or equal to today's price.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
Input: [100, 80, 60, 70, 60, 75, 85]
Output: [1, 1, 1, 2, 1, 4, 6]
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= price <= 10^5
- At most 10^4 calls to next
````
