Evaluate an arithmetic expression given in Reverse Polish Notation using integers and + - \* /.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
Input: ["2", "1", "+", "3", "*"]
Output: 9
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= len(tokens) <= 10^4
- Division truncates toward zero; no division by zero
````
