Decode strings of the form k\[encoded\_string\], allowing nesting, into the expanded plaintext.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
Input: "3[a]2[bc]"
Output: "aaabcbc"
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= len(s) <= 30
- k is positive and digits may span multiple characters
````
