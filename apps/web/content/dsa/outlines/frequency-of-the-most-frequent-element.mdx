You are given an integer array nums and an integer k. In one operation you may
increment any element by 1, at most k total increments across all operations. Return
the maximum achievable frequency of any single value after the operations.

## Examples

````text
Example 1:
    Input: nums = [1, 2, 4], k = 5
    Output: 3
Example 2:
    Input: nums = [1, 4, 8, 13], k = 5
    Output: 2
````

## Constraints

````text
- 1 <= len(nums) <= 10^5
- 1 <= nums[i], k <= 10^5
````
