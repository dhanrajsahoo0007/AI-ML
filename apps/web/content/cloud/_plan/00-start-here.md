- Diagrams:
  1. animated stepper `animated/cloud/queue-topic-stream` — steps: (1) Queue: producer → queue → two workers; packets split between workers / fails: one message, one worker; (2) Topic: producer → topic → billing, email, analytics each receive every packet / fails: nothing lost but nothing replayable; (3) Stream: producer → partitioned log, two consumer groups with their own offsets, rewind arrow / fails: parallelism capped by partition count; (4) **Worker crashes mid-message** (failure step): worker A dims after taking a packet, the queue redelivers to worker B, the duplicate is marked, idempotency key on the consumer absorbs it.
