## Pick your path

Every path uses the same pages. Only the order and the stopping point change.

- **From zero.** You have never opened a cloud console, and you want to design a small web app. Read [Path A](#path-a-from-zero).
- **Interview prep.** You know what a server and a database are, and an interview is weeks away. Read [Path B](#path-b-interview-prep).
- **Certification-minded.** You want broad coverage, level by level. Read [Path C](#path-c-by-level).

## Path A: From zero

This path follows Brightpost, the small online shop in every story, in the order it grew. It ends with a **three-tier app**: a front door for traffic, a tier of app servers, and a database tier. You can then draw that design on any of the three clouds.

1. [Cloud in 10 Minutes](/cloud/start-here/cloud-in-10-minutes) (10 min): the whole section as one story.
2. [What the Cloud Is](/cloud/fundamentals/what-is-cloud) (30 min): why rented capacity survives a sale day.
3. [Service Models](/cloud/fundamentals/service-models) (30 min): how much of the stack you run yourself.
4. [Regions, Zones and the Edge](/cloud/fundamentals/regions-zones-edge) (30 min): why you spread across <Term id="availability-zone">zones</Term>.
5. [Shared Responsibility](/cloud/fundamentals/shared-responsibility) (30 min): the security jobs that stay yours.
6. [Identity and Access](/cloud/fundamentals/identity-and-access) (35 min): how a cloud decides whether a request is allowed.
7. [Private Networks, Subnets and Routing](/cloud/networking/vpc-and-subnets) (35 min): one address range per tier.
8. [Firewalls](/cloud/networking/firewalls) (30 min): each tier talks only to the next one.
9. [Load Balancing](/cloud/networking/load-balancing) (30 min): one front door that skips dead servers.
10. [The Compute Spectrum](/cloud/compute/compute-spectrum) (30 min): machines, containers and functions on one line.
11. [Autoscaling](/cloud/compute/autoscaling) (30 min): servers added as load rises, removed after.
12. [Object, Block and File](/cloud/data/storage-types) (25 min): which storage holds photos, disks and shared folders.
13. [Managed Relational Databases](/cloud/data/managed-relational) (35 min): standbys and backups the provider runs.
14. [High Availability Inside One Region](/cloud/resilience/ha-patterns) (35 min): three tiers that survive a zone failure.
15. [Observability on the Cloud](/cloud/operations/observability-on-the-cloud) (30 min): see a fault before customers do.
16. [Practice: A Web App from Zero](/cloud/start-here/practice-web-app-from-zero) (60 min): design it yourself, then compare.

<Callout title="About 8.5 hours" tone="green" icon="clock">
  Sixteen pages, or about 13 days at 40 minutes a day. Keep [Service Equivalents](/cloud/start-here/service-equivalents) open to translate service names.
</Callout>

## Path B: Interview prep

Cloud questions in a design interview are mostly choices and failures. Where does this code run? What happens when a zone goes down? This path starts with the decision guides, then spends most of its time on failure. At the end you can defend each choice with a number.

1. [Service Equivalents](/cloud/start-here/service-equivalents) (15 min): answer on whichever cloud the interviewer uses.
2. [Choosing Compute](/cloud/start-here/choosing-compute) (25 min): three questions that place any workload.
3. [Queues vs Streams](/cloud/start-here/queues-vs-streams) (25 min): a queue, a topic or a stream, and when.
4. [Databases on Each Cloud](/cloud/start-here/databases-per-cloud) (25 min): one store per access pattern.
5. [Availability, SLAs and What Fails Together](/cloud/resilience/availability-math) (35 min): the arithmetic behind "four nines".
6. [Disaster Recovery](/cloud/resilience/dr-strategies) (35 min): <Term id="rpo">RPO</Term> and <Term id="rto">RTO</Term>, the two numbers behind every outage question.
7. [Multi-Region](/cloud/resilience/multi-region) (40 min): what a second <Term id="region">region</Term> changes and costs.
8. [Quotas, Throttling, Retries and Backoff](/cloud/resilience/quotas-and-retries) (30 min): why retries can worsen an outage.
9. [Managed Caches](/cloud/data/managed-caches) (30 min): cache patterns and how each fails.
10. [Load Balancing](/cloud/networking/load-balancing) (30 min): layer 4 or 7, one region or global.
11. [Identity and Access](/cloud/fundamentals/identity-and-access) (35 min): the security answer every design needs.
12. [Practice: Survive a Region Outage](/cloud/start-here/practice-survive-a-region-outage) (60 min): a timed design question.
13. [Practice: Lock Down a Leaky Account](/cloud/start-here/practice-lock-down-a-leaky-account) (60 min): a timed security question.

At each stop, read **How to say it in an interview** aloud. Then try the questions before opening the answers. The [question library](/interviews/cloud-architecture) groups every question by hub.

<Callout title="About 7.5 hours" tone="green" icon="clock">
  Thirteen pages, or about 11 hours with every question answered. In one week, read two pages a day and save both practice pages for the last day.
</Callout>

## Path C: By level

Cloud certifications come in three levels, and so does this path. Foundation is about words and the shape of a cloud. Associate is about building one workload well. Professional is about many accounts, many regions, migrations and cost.

**Foundation (about 4 hours).** You can explain what a cloud is, who secures what, and how the bill works.

1. [Cloud in 10 Minutes](/cloud/start-here/cloud-in-10-minutes): the map in one story.
2. [What the Cloud Is](/cloud/fundamentals/what-is-cloud): renting instead of owning.
3. [Service Models](/cloud/fundamentals/service-models): machines, platforms, functions, software.
4. [Regions, Zones and the Edge](/cloud/fundamentals/regions-zones-edge): where the machines are.
5. [Shared Responsibility](/cloud/fundamentals/shared-responsibility): the provider's jobs and yours.
6. [Identity and Access](/cloud/fundamentals/identity-and-access): who may do what.
7. [How Cloud Pricing Works](/cloud/fundamentals/pricing-basics): the lines on a bill.
8. [Service Equivalents](/cloud/start-here/service-equivalents): one idea, three names.

Then skim your cloud's hub: [AWS](/cloud/aws), [Google Cloud](/cloud/gcp) or [Azure](/cloud/azure).

**Associate (about 9 hours).** You can build, secure and scale one workload.

1. [Private Networks, Subnets and Routing](/cloud/networking/vpc-and-subnets): the address plan.
2. [Firewalls](/cloud/networking/firewalls): which traffic may pass.
3. [DNS on the Cloud](/cloud/networking/dns): public and private names.
4. [Load Balancing](/cloud/networking/load-balancing): one front door.
5. [The Compute Spectrum](/cloud/compute/compute-spectrum): where code runs.
6. [Autoscaling](/cloud/compute/autoscaling): servers that follow load.
7. [Serverless Functions](/cloud/compute/serverless-functions): triggers and cold starts.
8. [Object Storage](/cloud/data/object-storage): tiers, lifecycle, versioning.
9. [Managed Relational Databases](/cloud/data/managed-relational): standbys and replicas.
10. [Managed NoSQL](/cloud/data/managed-nosql): partition keys and capacity.
11. [Queues, Topics, Streams and Workflows](/cloud/data/streaming-and-messaging-services): events between services.
12. [High Availability Inside One Region](/cloud/resilience/ha-patterns): surviving a zone failure.
13. [Backups, Snapshots and Vaults](/cloud/resilience/backups-and-vaults): copies an attacker cannot delete.
14. [Encryption and Key Management](/cloud/resilience/encryption-and-keys): data at rest and in transit.
15. [Observability on the Cloud](/cloud/operations/observability-on-the-cloud): seeing what runs.

**Professional (about 7 hours).** You can design an estate of many accounts and regions, and move a live system into it.

1. [Accounts, Projects and Governance](/cloud/fundamentals/accounts-and-governance): guardrails across accounts.
2. [Landing Zones](/cloud/fundamentals/landing-zones): the estate before the first workload.
3. [Peering, Hub-and-Spoke and Transit](/cloud/networking/connecting-networks): joining many networks.
4. [VPN and Dedicated Connections](/cloud/networking/hybrid-connectivity): linking a data centre.
5. [Disaster Recovery](/cloud/resilience/dr-strategies): four tiers, priced against RPO and RTO.
6. [Multi-Region](/cloud/resilience/multi-region): global routing and replication.
7. [Database Migration](/cloud/data/database-migration): moving a live database.
8. [Migration and Modernization](/cloud/operations/migration-and-modernization): which apps move, and how far they change.
9. [CI/CD and Deployment Strategies](/cloud/operations/ci-cd-and-deployment-strategies): shipping changes safely.
10. [FinOps and the Cost Tools](/cloud/operations/finops-and-cost-tools): an owner for every line of the bill.

At this level, also answer every Senior question on these pages.
