> **Outline.** This topic is planned and will be written up in a future iteration.

**What it will cover:** the three ways a cloud says no: account quotas (how many of a thing), API rate limits (how fast you may ask) and per-resource throughput (how much a table or disk can serve). It explains why a retry storm turns a hiccup into an outage and how backoff, jitter and idempotency make retries safe.
