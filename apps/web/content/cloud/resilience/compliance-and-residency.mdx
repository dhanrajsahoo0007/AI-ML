**What it will cover:** what a compliance framework asks of a cloud design, why the provider's certificate does not make your app compliant, and the three ways to keep data in a place: pick the region, forbid other regions by policy, and draw a perimeter so data cannot leave by API. It explains residency (where data sits) versus sovereignty (whose law and whose keys).
