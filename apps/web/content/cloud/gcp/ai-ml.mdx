**Vertex AI** is Google Cloud's single AI platform. It serves Gemini and partner models, including **Claude**, through Model Garden; trains and serves your own models; and runs agents on **Agent Engine**. **Vertex AI Search** and the **RAG Engine** handle retrieval, and **TPUs** are Google's own accelerators for large training and inference jobs.

## TL;DR

- **Vertex AI** for LLM features: Gemini, Claude and open models behind one platform.
- **Agent Engine** to deploy agents with sessions, memory and tracing.
- **Vertex AI Search / RAG Engine** for grounding; **Vector Search** or AlloyDB for embeddings.
- **TPUs** for large training and inference where they beat GPUs on price-performance.

## Coming from AWS

| On AWS | On Google Cloud | What actually changes |
|---|---|---|
| Bedrock | Vertex AI Model Garden | Gemini is first-party; Claude available on both |
| Bedrock AgentCore | Vertex AI Agent Engine | Similar managed runtime for agents |
| Bedrock Knowledge Bases | Vertex AI Search / RAG Engine | Search product with built-in ranking |
| SageMaker AI | Vertex AI | Training, pipelines and endpoints in the same platform as foundation models |
| Trainium / Inferentia | TPUs | Mature JAX and PyTorch/XLA support |
