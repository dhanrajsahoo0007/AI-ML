## Report a copy of our content

If you find {{BRAND}} content copied, republished, sold or used to train an AI model somewhere else, please tell us. Email **{{SECURITY_EMAIL}}** or **{{FOUNDER_EMAIL}}** with the subject "Copied content" and include:
- the link to the copy, and a screenshot if you have one;
- the {{BRAND}} page it was copied from, if you know it.

We send takedown notices to the site, its hosting provider and search engines, and we may suspend any account the copy is traced to.

Your notice should include:
1. your name, address, email address and phone number;
2. a description of the work you believe has been infringed;
3. the exact URL of each page on {{BRAND}} where the material appears;
4. a statement that you believe in good faith that the use isn't authorised by you, your agent or the law;
5. a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the owner of the right, or authorised to act for the owner;
6. your physical or electronic signature.

## How we handle notices

- **United States (DMCA, 17 U.S.C. § 512):** we respond to notices that meet the requirements above. If we remove material, we tell the person who provided it, and they may send a counter-notice.
- **India (Information Technology Act, 2000 and IT Rules, 2021):** our grievance contact acknowledges a complaint within **24 hours** and resolves it within **15 days**. Where the rules require, we remove or disable access to the material within the time they set.
- **Elsewhere:** we act on valid notices under the law that applies, including the EU Digital Services Act.

We usually remove or disable access to material that is clearly infringing within **2 business days** of a complete notice.

## Counter-notices

If material you provided was removed and you believe that was a mistake, reply to our removal email with your name and contact details, the removed material and where it appeared, a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe it was removed by mistake, and your consent to the jurisdiction of the courts of **{{SELLER_CITY_STATE}}, India** (or your local courts where the law gives you that right). Unless the original complainant tells us they have started legal action, we may restore the material.

## False notices

Please only send notices for genuine claims. Knowingly false notices or counter-notices may lead to liability for damages.
