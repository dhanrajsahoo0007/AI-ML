## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can:
- **access** your data and get a copy; **Account → Data → Export** gives you a JSON download
- **correct** inaccurate data
- **delete** your account and data; **Account → Data → Delete account** (some records are kept as described in section 6)
- **object to** or **restrict** some processing, and **withdraw consent** (e.g. unsubscribe from the newsletter)
- **data portability**
- under the **DPDP Act**: a summary of the data we process and who we share it with, correction and erasure, grievance redressal, and nominating someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated
- under the **CCPA/CPRA**: know, delete, correct, and not be discriminated against for exercising your rights; we don't sell or share data

To use any right, email **privacy@{{DOMAIN}}** from your account email. We respond within **30 days**, or sooner where the law requires. We may need to verify your identity. You can also complain to your data-protection authority: for example, your EU supervisory authority, the UK ICO, or the **Data Protection Board of India** (after first contacting us).
