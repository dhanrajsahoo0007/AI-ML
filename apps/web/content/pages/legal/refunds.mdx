## When we always refund

The no-refund rule doesn't apply in these cases, and we'll refund you in full:
- **Charged by mistake:** a duplicate charge, a charge after you cancelled, or a charge you didn't authorise (please also tell your bank about unauthorised charges).
- **We couldn't deliver:** you paid but couldn't access Pro because of a problem on our side that we didn't fix within 7 days of you telling us.
- **The law requires it:** wherever mandatory consumer law in your country gives you a refund right that can't be waived, it applies.

To ask, email **support@{{DOMAIN}}** (or **dhanrajsahoo.work@gmail.com**) from your account email with the subject "Billing issue". We reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method; banks usually take 5–10 business days to show them.

## Cancelling your subscription

- Cancel any time from **Account → Billing → Manage subscription**, or email us and we'll cancel it for you.
- Cancelling **stops future renewals**. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again.
- We email a reminder **7 days before every yearly renewal**, so you can cancel in time.
- Cancelling doesn't refund the current period.

## EU, EEA and UK customers

You normally have a 14-day right to withdraw from online purchases. For digital content that starts immediately, that right ends once access begins, **if you agree to it**. At checkout you'll be asked to:
1. agree that Pro access starts **immediately**, and
2. acknowledge that you therefore **lose your right of withdrawal**.

We keep a record of that consent. If you don't agree, you can't complete the purchase.

## Chargebacks

If something looks wrong with a charge, please **contact us first**; we fix genuine billing errors quickly. A chargeback for a valid purchase may lead to the account being suspended while it's resolved.
