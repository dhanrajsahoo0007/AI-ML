Some interview questions in **Interview Practice** and the company interview-loop summaries in **Company Questions** are adapted from **[AI Engineering Interview Questions Company Wise](https://github.com/pallavi-shekhar/ai-engineering-interview-questions-company-wise)** by **Outcome School**, Copyright © 2026 Outcome School, licensed under the **[Apache License, Version 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)**.

Changes we made: we selected only AI and ML questions, edited some wording for clarity, merged near-duplicates, normalised company names, filed questions into our own categories, rewrote the roles and loop summaries in our own words, and **wrote every answer ourselves**. "Further reading" links point to Outcome School's own articles and videos, which are referenced, not copied.
