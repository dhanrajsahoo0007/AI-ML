**Effective date:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

Keeping learners' accounts and payments safe matters to us. If you find a vulnerability, please tell us privately so we can fix it before anyone is harmed. Our machine-readable contact is at [security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt).

## How to report

Email **{{SECURITY_EMAIL}}** (or **{{FOUNDER_EMAIL}}**) with the subject "Security report" and include:
- what the issue is and where it is (URL, endpoint or feature);
- steps to reproduce it, and a proof of concept if you have one;
- the impact you think it has;
- your name or handle, if you'd like to be credited.

Please write in English. We'll confirm we've received your report within **2 business days**, keep you updated while we work on it, and tell you when it's fixed.

## In scope

- the website at **{{DOMAIN}}** and its subdomains run by us
- sign-in, accounts, the paywall and access to Pro content
- our checkout pages and billing webhooks (payment processing itself belongs to our reseller, {{MERCHANT_OF_RECORD}}, which runs its own disclosure programme)

## Out of scope

- denial-of-service or load testing, and spam or social engineering of our team or users
- physical attacks, and attacks on other people's accounts or devices
- third-party services we use (Cloudflare, Google, {{MERCHANT_OF_RECORD}} and others): please report to them directly
- reports from automated scanners with no shown impact, missing best-practice headers with no exploit, clickjacking on pages with no sensitive actions, and self-XSS
- ways to get around the copy deterrents in the browser (they're a deterrent, not a security boundary), unless they expose Pro content to users who haven't paid

## Rules for testing

- Only test against accounts you own, or with the account holder's permission.
- Don't access, change or delete other people's data. If you reach personal data by accident, stop, don't keep it, and tell us.
- Don't degrade the Service for others; keep automated testing slow and gentle.
- Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before you tell anyone else. We aim to fix serious issues within **90 days**.

## Safe harbour

If you act in good faith and follow these rules, we **won't take legal action against you** or ask the authorities to, for your research. We consider such research authorised under computer-misuse and anti-circumvention laws, and we'll say so if someone else brings a claim about it. If you're unsure whether something is allowed, ask us first at {{SECURITY_EMAIL}}.

We're a small team and can't pay bug bounties yet, but we're happy to thank you publicly with your permission.
