### Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from Account → Billing in a couple of clicks. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.

### What's your refund policy?

Pro gives you instant access to digital content, so **all sales are final**. We always refund billing errors such as duplicate charges or charges after cancelling. Try the free articles in every section before you buy. [Read the policy](/legal/refunds).

### Do you offer regional pricing?

Yes. Prices are adjusted for many countries based on the billing country of your payment method. The checkout shows your exact price.

### Which payment methods do you accept?

Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay, Google Pay and, in many countries, local methods such as UPI in India and PayPal where available. Payments are handled securely by our reseller, {{MERCHANT_OF_RECORD}}, and we never see your card details.

### Will I get an invoice?

Yes. A receipt or invoice is emailed after every payment. Business buyers can add a VAT/GST ID at checkout.

### Does monthly renew automatically?

Yes. Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically until you cancel. We email a reminder before every yearly renewal.

### Is it suitable for students and younger learners?

Yes. {{BRAND}} is for learners of every age, and free content needs no account. Learners under 18 need a parent's or guardian's permission to create an account, and purchases are made by an adult.

### Can my team use one account?

Accounts are for one person. Team plans are coming later.
